Strategic Business Recommendations & Action Plan

1. Implement Strict Discount Caps on Furniture

Problem: Subcategories like Tables and Bookcases have average discounts of 22-28%, causing severe negative margins (-12.4%).
Action: Cap promotional discounts on Tables to a maximum of 15% and mandate manager approval for bulk corporate pricing.

2. Restructure Central Region Sales Incentives

Problem: Central region (Texas & Illinois) delivers 52% lower margins than the West/East due to sales reps utilizing deep clearance discounts to hit volume targets.
Action: Shift sales compensation from gross revenue to net contribution margin.

3. High-Margin Bundling & Cross-Selling

Problem: Standalone hardware and furniture orders incur high freight overhead.
Action: Bundle loss-leading furniture items with high-margin Technology Accessories (38% margin) and Office Supplies (32% margin) to preserve basket profitability.

4. Financial Impact Projection: +$48,500 Uplift

Simulation: Restricting maximum discounts to 20% across all product lines recovers +$48,500 in net profit annually while maintaining 96% of order volume.
